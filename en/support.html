Frequently asked questions

How do I create a new habit?

From the home screen, tap the “+” button to open the wizard. You'll go through four quick steps: name, icon and color, frequency, and an optional reminder. Tap “Finish” on the last step and your habit appears in the list.

Can I sync my habits across devices?

No. Habrio is intentionally offline-first and privacy-first — your habits live only on the device where you created them. There are no accounts and no cloud sync of your habits.

I'm not getting notifications. What should I do?

Open your phone's Settings app and make sure Habrio is allowed to send notifications: on iOS, go to Notifications → Habrio; on Android, look for App info → Habrio → Notifications. Then check that the habit you want a reminder for has a reminder time configured (you can verify this in the habit's detail screen).

How do reminders work?

Habrio supports two kinds of reminders. Per-habit reminders fire at the specific time you set when creating or editing a habit. Daily nudges are gentle, organic notifications that appear at most once a day, only if you have pending habits — they never spam you.

Where is my data stored, and how do I delete it?

Your habits, their names and your history are stored locally on your device in a private database. So that reminders arrive on time even when the app is closed, Habrio sends our server only what reminders need (time, frequency and time zone), with anonymous identifiers for the device and each habit — never your habit names. To delete everything, just uninstall the app: local data is removed right away, and the reminder times are deleted from the server automatically after 30 days without use.

What languages are supported?

Habrio is available in 6 languages: English, Portuguese, Spanish, French, German and Russian. The app uses your device's language automatically; in any other language it appears in English.

Does Habrio collect any data?

Habrio collects fully anonymous usage events (such as screen views and habit creation events) for analytics. It never collects your habit names, notes, completion timestamps, email, name, or any personally identifiable information. See our privacy policy for details.

How do I report a bug or request a feature?

Send an email to michelbueno.apps@gmail.com with a short description and, if possible, your phone model and OS version (iOS or Android). Screenshots help a lot.
