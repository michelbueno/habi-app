Privacy Policy

How Habi handles your data — what stays on your device, what we collect anonymously, and what we never collect.

Introduction

Welcome to Habi. This policy describes how the app handles your information: what stays on your device, what we collect anonymously to improve the app, and what we never collect.

Locally stored data

Your habit information is stored exclusively on your device in a local SQLite database:

  • Names and settings of your habits (frequency, days of the week)
  • Completion records per date
  • Configured reminder times

Your habit names and completion history are never transmitted to any server and are permanently deleted when you uninstall the app. The only exception is the reminder data described below.

Data sent for reminders

So that reminders arrive on time even when the app is closed, Habi sends our server (Google Firebase) only what is needed to schedule them:

  • An anonymous installation identifier and the device's time zone
  • For each habit with a reminder: a random habit identifier, the reminder time and its frequency
  • The device's notification token, used to deliver the reminder

Habit names and content are never sent. Access uses anonymous Firebase authentication, with no account, email or password. This data is used only to send reminders, and each reminder sent is recorded anonymously in the usage data. It is deleted from the server automatically after 30 days without using the app.

Usage data collected

We collect anonymous and aggregated usage data to understand how the app is used and improve it. This data does not identify you personally and does not include the names or content of your habits.

What is collected:

  • Navigation: which screens are visited in the app
  • Habit actions: creation, update, deletion and completion — including metadata such as chosen icon, frequency (daily/weekly), days of the week, and whether a reminder is active
  • Consistency milestones: when a streak milestone is reached (e.g., 7, 14 or 30 days), including the streak value and habit icon
  • Notifications: whether a notification was delivered or opened, the message template used, and aggregated counts of pending/completed habits on that day
  • Performance: screen load times and number of habits when loading the list
  • Stability: app error and crash reports

Data we never collect

  • Name, email, phone number, or any personally identifiable information
  • The names or content of your habits
  • Geographic location
  • Health or biometric data
  • Data from other apps
  • Payment information

System permissions

Habi requests only notification permission, used exclusively to send habit reminders you configure. No other system access — camera, microphone, location, contacts, or files — is requested.

Data retention

Usage data is retained according to the policies of the third-party services listed above. Reminder data is deleted from the server after 30 days without using the app. Local habit data is deleted when the app is uninstalled.

Children

Habi is not directed at children under 13. The app collects anonymous usage data through third-party services (Firebase, Amplitude and Datadog), without a parental consent mechanism in place. Parents or guardians who identify use by children under 13 should uninstall the application.

Data deletion

Local habit data can be removed at any time by uninstalling the app; after that, reminder data is deleted from the server automatically within 30 days. To request earlier deletion, or deletion of usage data collected by third-party services, contact us at the email below or refer to the Firebase, Amplitude and Datadog policies linked above.
